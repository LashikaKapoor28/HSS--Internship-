Biocultural Restoration

Healing both nature and culture together - restoring ecosystems while revitalizing traditional knowledge and practices.

Back to Our Work

Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK)

We integrate indigenous knowledge systems into restoration plans, working alongside community elders to map sacred groves, water sources, and land-use boundaries that have sustained ecosystems for generations.

Ecocultural Participatory Mapping

Communities actively map their landscapes - identifying natural features, sacred sites, and areas in need of restoration. This participatory process ensures local voices shape every restoration plan.

Cultural Heritage Restoration

We work to preserve and restore cultural heritage sites including sacred spaces and traditional landmarks, recognizing that a thriving culture is inseparable from a thriving landscape.
