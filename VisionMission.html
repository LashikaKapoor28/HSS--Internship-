Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Guided by our values, we work towards a future where nature and culture thrive together.

Our Vision

A resilient and equitable world, where the interconnectedness of nature and culture are celebrated and protected for generations to come. We envision a transformative landscape where women, marginalized groups, and diverse communities actively participate in shaping a regenerative and harmonious future.

Our Mission

To create a transformative landscape where women, marginalized groups, and diverse communities actively participate in shaping a regenerative and harmonious future for both people and the planet. We believe that local communities have the power, resources, skills, and people to take charge of their own development.
