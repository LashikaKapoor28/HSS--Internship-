Who We Are

We believe local communities have the power to lead their own development

Homegrown Sustainability Solutions (HSS) is an innovative non-profit local organization modeling a different kind of leadership for landscape restoration. We strive to create a transformative landscape where women, marginalized groups, and diverse communities actively participate in shaping a regenerative and harmonious future.
